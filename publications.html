Geospatial and Deep Learning Approaches for Modeling Floodwater Depth in Urbanized Areas
Remote Sensing · MDPI
A model that reads aerial images and terrain to work out how deep floodwater is, accurate to within about 3 inches.
Floodwater depth estimation is essential for disaster response and infrastructure planning yet remains challenging in urban areas with limited gage and hydrological data. This study presents a deep learning-based framework grounded in the hydrostatic equilibrium principle to estimate flood depth using a remote sensing approach. ResNet18 outperformed deeper models, achieving RMSE of 0.71 ft, MAE of 0.23 ft, SSIM ≈ 99% and R² ≈ 94%.
Inundation2Depth: A Multi-Source Dataset for Floodwater Depth Estimation in Urban Areas
Data in Brief · Elsevier
An open dataset of 5,925 flood images from 12 flooded areas in the Carolinas, free for other researchers to use.
Floods impose severe risks in urban areas, yet operational mapping often stops at inundation extent rather than depth. Inundation2Depth pairs inundation extent-depth labels derived from aerial imagery and LiDAR-based DTMs under hydrostatic assumptions. The dataset encompasses 12 flood-affected areas across North and South Carolina covering 24,649.88 acres with 5,925 overlapping tiles.
Multi-Resolution Data Fusion for Resilient Flood Mapping
IEEE Access
Combining drone photos with radar maps floods better than either one on its own.
This study presents a comparative analysis of flood mapping using multimodal sensor fusion and semantic segmentation. Integrating high-resolution UAV RGB imagery with SAR data from Sentinel-1 and UAVSAR, a modified U-Net architecture handles multimodal stacked inputs. The combination of RGB UAV with UAVSAR VV polarization at 5 m resolution achieves the highest performance (mean IoU: 84.9%), outperforming RGB UAV alone (77.8%).
Real-Time Traffic Insights with Physics-Informed Neural Networks
IEEE Access
Predicting traffic flow by teaching the model the physics of how vehicles move.
This work integrates the Aw-Rascle macroscopic traffic model with physics-informed neural networks and large language models to deliver real-time traffic insights, improving prediction over purely data-driven baselines.
Dark Development: Building Density and Electricity Provision in Ghana's Urban Areas
Remote Sensing Applications · Elsevier
Using night-time satellite images to show that 21% of Kumasi is densely built but poorly served with electricity.
This study combines satellite-derived building density with VIIRS nighttime light data to identify areas of Ghana's cities that are densely built yet underserved by electricity infrastructure, using a U-Net regression model to estimate building composition from imagery.
DeepFlood for Inundated Vegetation: A High-Resolution Dataset for Flood Mapping
Scientific Data · Nature
An open dataset for spotting flooding hidden under trees and crops, where ordinary satellite images cannot see the water.
DeepFlood provides a high-resolution, multi-source dataset for accurate flood mapping and segmentation in inundated vegetation, combining SAR and optical imagery to support convolutional neural network training where water is obscured by vegetation canopy.
Pixels to Insights: Deep Learning for Floodwater Depth Mapping in Settlement Areas
IEEE IGARSS 2025 · Brisbane, Australia
Mapping flood depths of up to 4.8 feet in neighbourhoods hit by Hurricane Matthew.
This paper applies a U-Net with a ResNet-18 encoder to estimate floodwater depth across settlement areas, achieving RMSE ≈ 0.11 and predicting maximum depths of 4.8 ft in Hurricane Matthew study areas.

Advanced Geo-Data Analytics and AI for 3D Flood Mapping to Protect Built Assets
ISPRS Annals · Dubai, UAE
Building 3D flood maps so planners can see which buildings and roads are at risk.
This study integrates LiDAR, aerial imagery and deep learning to produce 3D flood maps for infrastructure risk assessment, with a U-Net model reaching approximately 93% accuracy in delineating flood extent over built assets.
Flood Impact Risk Mapping from a 3D Perspective: Hurricane Matthew
IEEE IGARSS 2024 · Athens, Greece
After Hurricane Matthew, 26% of buildings and 66% of roads in Greenville, NC were affected by flooding.
This paper develops a 3D flood map of Hurricane Matthew's impact in settlement areas using UAV high-resolution imagery and LiDAR-derived digital elevation models, quantifying flood-affected land, buildings and roads to support emergency response and adaptation planning.

Urban Growth and Land Surface Temperature Dynamics: Lessons from Ghana
Theoretical and Empirical Research in Urban Management
Tracking how cities in Ghana grew over time, and how much hotter that made them.
This study analyses the relationship between urban growth and land surface temperature dynamics in Ghana using Landsat imagery, showing how expanding built-up areas drive urban heat island effects.
