CV — 01
Featured · Active Research
Physics-Informed Deep Learning for Urban Flood Depth Prediction

A physics-informed framework for CNNs and transformer-based models, that encodes hydrostatic terrain constraints directly into the loss function — ensuring predictions respect the physical principle that water flows downhill and depth correlates with terrain elevation.

Outcome
Preliminary expirements show 0.67 ft RMSE · 0.23 ft MAE on held-out test set. Custom terrain penalty weights enforce physically consistent predictions under hydrostatic conditions.

Inundation2Depth: Geospatial Benchmark Dataset

Curated and built the Inundation2Depth dataset — high-resolution geospatial tiles linking flood inundation extent to measured floodwater depth across 12 hurricane-affected areas in the Carolinas, validated with HEC-RAS hydrodynamic modeling.

Outcome
5,925 tiles · 24,649 acres · 256×256 px patches available in raw and normalized versions. Published in Elsevier Data in Brief and available on Zenodo.

GIS — 01
Featured · Published
3D Flood Depth Mapping & Risk Assessment with ArcPy

Developed a 3D flood map of Hurricane Matthew's impact in settlement areas using UAV high-resolution imagery and LiDAR data. Applied ArcPy spatial statistics and geoprocessing to estimate flood depth from DEMs and delineate flood extent.

Outcome
Mapped flood-risk levels across affected communities. ~32% of developed land, 26% of buildings, and 66% of roads in Greenville, NC found to be flood-affected. Informs emergency response and long-term adaptation planning.
