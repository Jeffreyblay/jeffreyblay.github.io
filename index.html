About

I use satellite images to measure floods.

I am a PhD candidate at NC A&T State University, finishing in December 2026, with a master's from Yale and a bachelor's from the University of Ghana. My research teaches computers to read aerial and satellite images and work out how deep floodwater is, so cities can see which homes and roads are at risk.
