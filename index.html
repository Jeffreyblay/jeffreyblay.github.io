About

I use satellite images to measure floods.

I am a PhD candidate at NC A&T State University, finishing in December 2026, with a master's from Yale and a bachelor's from the University of Ghana. My research teaches computers to read aerial and satellite images and work out how deep floodwater is, so cities can see which homes and roads are at risk.

Research

What I work on

Reading images with deep learning

Teaching models to estimate flood depth, land cover and disaster damage from aerial, satellite and LiDAR images, with the physics of how water behaves built into the model.

Combining different sensors

Merging radar and optical satellite images so floods can be mapped accurately even through cloud and at night.

Building the data pipelines

Python pipelines that clean and prepare very large map and satellite datasets, plus open datasets other researchers can use.

Measuring risk to infrastructure

Mapping buildings, urban heat and electricity access across African cities to show which communities are underserved.
