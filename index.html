About Me

Turning pixels into operational intelligence.

I'm a PhD candidate at NC A&T State University,in Applied Science and Technology (expected December 2026). I hold an MS in Environmental Data Science from Yale School of the Environment and a BS in Geography from the University of Ghana.

I build geospatial AI systems that turn raw Earth data into real-world decisions. Over the past 6+ years, I have developed scalable machine learning frameworks using satellite/UAV imagery and other spatial data. My PhD research work focuses on urban flood prediction, where I design physics-informed deep learning frameworks to improve how flood depth risk is quantified and acted on.

I am comfortable across the full stack- data engineering, modeling, and deployment, and have experience translating research into production-ready pipelines and scalable analytics platforms.

My broader goal is to advance reliable, scalable methods that bridge geospatial AI research and real-world applications.
