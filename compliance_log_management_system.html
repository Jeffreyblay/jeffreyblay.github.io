Compliance Log Management System

A free tool for tracking oil quality-control sample records: what was logged, where it is stored, when it expires and who is responsible. It runs on one office computer, so no internet and no subscription are needed.

Download free View the code
Free forever · Open source (MIT) · Windows · Works offline
What it does

Everything you need, nothing you do not

Keep every record

Add, edit, view and delete product records. Every column from your existing spreadsheet is supported.

Find anything fast

Search by product number, version, initials, cabinet or shelf. Filter by status.

Never miss a due date

Records are flagged automatically as active, due soon or overdue, so nothing expires unnoticed.

Daily email alerts

An email goes out each morning listing anything due soon or overdue. Set it up once.

Import your spreadsheet

Upload your existing CSV or Excel file and the system matches your column names for you.

Share with the team

Runs on one computer and opens in any browser on the same office network. No accounts to manage.

Setup

Running in four steps

01

Download and unzip

Extract the file to C:\ComplianceLog\ on the computer that will host it.

02

Install Python

Get it from python.org and tick "Add Python to PATH" on the first screen.

03

Double-click start.bat

The first run installs what it needs and takes about a minute.

04

Import your file

Open localhost:5000 in a browser, click Import and upload your log. Done.

Data

Matches your existing log

Every field from your spreadsheet is supported, so you can import what you already have without changing it.

Input date
When the product was logged.
Product number
The unique number for each entry.
Version
Reference number within the product.
Visibility
Active or archived.
Cabinet
Where the sample is stored.
Shelf
The exact shelf in that cabinet.
Expiry date
Drives the active, due soon and overdue flags.
Initials
Who is responsible for the record.
